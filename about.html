ABOUT THE LESSON

Media and Information Literacy

Learn how to understand media messages and use information responsibly.

What is Media Inforamtion Literacy?

Media and Information Literacy (MIL) is the ability to access, understand, evaluate, and use information from different media. It helps us decide if information is reliable and notice content that may be misleading.

Why is it important?

People use social media to get news and communicate with others. However, not every post online is true. MIL reminds us to check sources, compare information, notice bias, and think before sharing.

A few things to remember

  1. Read beyond the headline.
  2. Check the author, date, and original source.
  3. Compare the information with other reliable sources.
  4. Do not share a claim if you cannot verify it.

MEDIA ELEMENTS

Text, font, and color

Media creators use these elements to communicate information and influence how people understand a message.

T

Text

Text gives the audience the main message. Clear and accurate wording makes information easier to understand.

Font

Font affects readability and mood. Simple fonts are often easier to read, especially for longer information.

●

Color

Color attracts attention and can suggest a feeling or meaning. Use colors that keep the message easy to read.

How can you recognize possible misinformation?

  • The headline sounds shocking or tries to make you angry.
  • The post does not provide a trustworthy source.
  • The photo or video has no clear context or date.
  • Other reliable sources do not confirm the claim.

These signs do not automatically prove that a post is false. They mean you should investigate before trusting or sharing it.
