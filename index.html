Why does media literacy matter?

We see posts, videos, photos, and news every day. Knowing how to check them helps us make better decisions.

01

Check the source

Look at who posted the information and whether the source is trustworthy.

02

Verify the facts

Compare the claim with reliable sources before believing it or sharing it.

03

Share responsibly

Think about how a post could affect others before you send or repost it.

BE A RESPONSIBLE MEDIA USER

Pause, check, then share.

A few seconds of checking can help stop misinformation from spreading.
